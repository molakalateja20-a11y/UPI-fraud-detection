📚 Project Mathematical Formulations

To explain the core AI and analytical logic to the HOD, the system is modeled on three key statistical and mathematical pillars:

1. Random Forest Split Criterion (Gini Impurity)

Decision trees split nodes by minimizing impurity. The probability $p_i$ is the fraction of transactions of class $i$ in a split node $T$:

Gini(T) = 1 - ∑i=1C (pi)2
2. Ensemble Classifier Prediction Probability

The overall probability is the mean prediction of $B$ independent decision tree models ($T_b$) in the forest:

P(y = 1 | x) = (1 / B) × ∑b=1B Pb(y = 1 | x)
3. Transaction Amount Anomaly (Z-Score metric)

To capture anomalous amounts compared to reference UPI transactional averages ($\mu$ = ₹5,000, $\sigma$ = ₹8,000):

Anomaly(x) = | x - μ | / σ
